The application now selects an exact PostgreSQL lookup for a single bare or labelled SKU, a bounded catalog search for multiple SKUs or natural product browsing, and the prior handle's current catalog row for “this product” follow-ups. A price-bound request no longer requires the filler word “options” to appear in a product name. A category-only follow-up omits unrequested variant prices. The local model still receives authorized RAG context, the checked catalog result, and the final prompt, but an unchecked model introduction cannot add product facts or invented locations to the rendered catalog result. Clear product requests skip the unreliable extra model tool-choice call. Other company questions may still request a bounded follow-up search through the guarded tool path.

Citation markers now refer to a contiguous list of distinct source IDs, so repeated excerpts from the same source cannot leave a new answer with an orphan `[3]`. Previously saved answers are not rewritten. The direct “What is Sardars?” probe had asserted that Sardars was a “shoemaker” with an About Us citation, although none of the three selected excerpts supported that occupation. That exact identity request now returns the selected About Us self-description after the model turn. The customer-opinion probe copied FAQ metadata, repeated itself, and earlier omitted price criticism. The narrow unqualified “what do customers/people say about Sardars” request now returns the exact authorized FAQ answer, including its limited-sample caveat and price criticism, after the model turn. Mention of *price criticism* alone is treated as sentiment rather than a current price assertion; a separate numeric or price claim remains guarded. These are targeted fallbacks for observed failures, not a general proof that generated company claims are grounded.
