# E5 retrieval and grounded-answer checkpoint — 2026-10-22

E5 uses the **same uploaded E4 corpus**, frozen questions and configured local `qwen3:4b-instruct-2507-q4_K_M` model. The starting E4 evaluation was 13/21 uploaded-source cases, 31/40 full-site cases, 25/26 chat cases and 6/7 staff-role seed cases. The historical six full-site failures were retained. The private, Git-ignored reports and source snapshot are in `data/extracted/e5_20261021/`; case definitions and evaluator checks were not relaxed to raise scores. Results are local engineering checks, not an independent sample of customer questions or a business accuracy approval.

## Diagnosis and accepted changes

All 24 source-linked full-site cases had their expected answer phrases in registered, ready source snapshots. Five expected pages were absent from the semantic top-32 pool, so ingestion coverage was not the cause of those particular misses. A bounded Qdrant text-match scroll now adds at most 16 role-prefiltered candidates to the existing semantic pool; PostgreSQL still authorizes every candidate before it can enter an answer. A short answer-bearing sentence gets a reranking boost over scattered keyword hits in long SEO copy. This recovered the saved project, article and product-source misses without reindexing.

The remaining saved failures were answer selection: an authorized sibling product was chosen for bathroom storage, a seat's general durability sentence displaced its material list, and two bathtub descriptions yielded trailing variant fragments. Source-order and evidence-selection changes prefer the specific matching product, enumerated materials and a descriptive product sentence. The saved article questions now cite the matching article. The six retained failures — `mirror`, `seat_materials`, `mirror_paraphrase`, `fittings_materials_article`, `sustainable_toilets_article` and `maintenance_article` — passed the full-corpus rerun; the nine cases that failed on the expanded E4 corpus also passed. This is a new-corpus evaluation result, not a claim that the original E2 corpus was restored or unchanged.

For descriptive company questions with no complete sentence-level term match, a small, authorized passage set can reach the local model. The application retains the initial route and source authorization. The model receives the shared system rules and numbered untrusted excerpts, then its short answer is checked for citation identity, source-word anchors, new numbers, negation and sensitive business claims; inadequate output abstains. Exact FAQ pairs and clearly supported direct extracts keep their fast paths. A single-product identification with an already sufficient, cited excerpt now returns that excerpt directly: an earlier 4B call took 171.5 seconds in the chat replay and ultimately returned the same product evidence. This narrow direct answer avoids spending model time where it adds no information. Explicit current SKU, price and stock questions still use PostgreSQL and the bounded product tools.

The E4 uploaded-source failures were separately traced to FAQ paraphrase selection, an overbroad `address` claim guard, qualified `availability`/`stock` caveats, missed research wording, and a generic product sibling. Narrow claim qualification, guarded close FAQ matching, and supporting research-sentence selection fixed those cases. A current Google rating remains a **cited statement that the FAQ did not verify a live value**, never a numeric live rating. Current hours, policy, delivery promise, charity claim and CEO questions still abstain. Role/source revocation and prompt-injection controls passed in the retained chat controls and focused backend tests.

## Chunk-size decision

The live policy remains 100 words with 15-word overlap for ordinary text; the earlier FAQ fix keeps short labelled Q/A pairs intact. E5 added an opt-in paragraph/FAQ-aware chunker and a reproducible **offline** comparison over all 991 saved ready source records. The baseline produced 5,825 chunks; the shadow policy produced 6,184 (**359 more, 6.2%**), changing 285 sources. All 24 source-linked frozen questions had their expected answer phrases in one chunk under both policies, with the same aggregate maximum query-term overlap (74). There was no measured locality benefit to justify replacement embeddings, index writes or source updates. `e5_chunk_compare.py` and `chunk_compare.json` record the proxy comparison. This did **not** run vector ranking or answer-generation latency on a shadow index; those cannot be claimed as improvements or regressions. No source was reindexed and no data volume was reset.
